The Manifesto

01 / NO RETAINERS FOR SHOW

If a channel stalls, we kill it that week - not "let's revisit next quarter." Even when that means a smaller invoice for us. We'd rather lose the line item than waste your money.

02 / NO IMPRESSION THEATER

Reach and impressions don't pay your bills.

Big reach numbers look great in a slide and terrible on a bank statement. We track cost per lead, cost per sale, and how much of your revenue actually traces back to the ad - the stuff your CFO would ask about.

03 / NO SLOW DECISIONS

We ship changes in days, not quarters.

Most agencies test a new headline once a month if you're lucky. We're swapping creative and rewriting copy that's underperforming within days - the algorithm doesn't wait for your quarterly review, so neither do we.

04 / AI PLUS HUMAN, NOT AI INSTEAD OF HUMAN

The models spot the pattern. A person decides what to do with it.

We run AI across every account's bid signals, creative fatigue, and audience drop-off - the stuff that'd take a person days to catch by hand. But creative direction, positioning, and what actually gets scaled or killed still goes through someone who's run ad accounts for a living, not a dashboard left on autopilot.
