Ranking for the right words is the job

Most SEO reports are a wall of keyword positions nobody asked for. We start by figuring out what a #1 ranking is actually worth to your business, then work backward from there - which pages, which searches, which buyer intent.

Technical fixes come first: crawl errors, page speed, site structure. Content strategy only starts once the foundation can actually support it. Then we build pages that answer the specific questions your buyers are typing into Google, not filler stuffed with keywords for their own sake.

What a month actually looks like

Technical audit and fix list in week one. Content calendar built around real search demand, not guesswork, by week two. Ongoing on-page optimization and link building from week three onward, with reporting that shows organic traffic and conversions - not just impressions or a vanity ranking chart.

The honest timeline

SEO is slower than paid media by nature. Expect early technical wins in the first few weeks, and meaningful ranking movement from month two to three, compounding from there. If you need pipeline this quarter, we'll tell you to pair it with Paid Media rather than wait on SEO alone - that's a conversation we have upfront, not six months in.
